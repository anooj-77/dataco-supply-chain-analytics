## Business Objectives

- Monitor sales and profitability performance.
- Compare product, category, customer-segment, and market results.
- Evaluate delivery performance and identify areas for further investigation.
- Present business metrics through interactive visualizations.

## Business Insights & Recommendations

### 1. Delivery Performance — Logistics Improvement

Business concern: Late deliveries can affect customer satisfaction and operational efficiency.

Recommended actions:

- Compare late-delivery rates across shipping methods, markets, and time periods.
- Investigate shipping methods or regions with consistently higher delay rates.
- Review order fulfilment and shipping performance to identify improvement opportunities.

Expected outcome: Better delivery monitoring, improved fulfilment decisions, and reduced delivery delays where the underlying causes can be addressed.

### 2. Sales & Profitability — Revenue Improvement

Business concern: High sales do not always translate into high profitability.

Recommended actions:

- Compare sales, profit, and profit margin across products and categories.
- Investigate products with high sales but relatively low margins.
- Review discount levels to understand their relationship with profitability.

Expected outcome: Better pricing and discount decisions, stronger profit awareness, and more informed sales strategies.

### 3. Product & Market Analysis — Performance Optimization

Business concern: Product and market performance may vary, creating opportunities for more focused business decisions.

Recommended actions:

- Identify leading and underperforming products and markets using verified dashboard results.
- Compare product-category profitability across markets.
- Prioritize further analysis of segments with declining performance or improvement potential.

Expected outcome: More focused marketing and inventory planning, better resource allocation, and improved visibility into business performance.

## Overall Conclusion

The three-page Power BI dashboard brings together sales, profitability, product and market analysis, and delivery performance. The recommendations demonstrate how dashboard metrics can support business investigation and decision-making.

# dataco-supply-chain-analytics
Interactive PowerBI dashboard analyzing Dataco supply chain sales, product and market performance, profitability, and delivery operations.

## DataCo Supply Chain Analytics | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard for analyzing sales performance, product and market trends, profitability, and delivery operations using the DataCo dataset.

## Dashboard Overview

### 1. Sales Overview

Summarizes key business KPIs, sales trends, category performance, customer segments, market performance, and delivery status.

## Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Data visualization and dashboard design
- KPI development and business performance analysis
- DAX and Power Query, where used in the project

## Business Objectives

- Monitor sales and profitability performance.
- Compare product, category, customer-segment, and market results.
- Evaluate delivery performance and identify areas for further investigation.
- Present business metrics through interactive visualizations.
